<h2>What Is a Medicare Annual Wellness Exam — And Why Does It Matter?</h2>

<p>One of the most underutilized Medicare benefits is the Annual Wellness Visit (AWV). Unlike a standard physical, the Medicare AWV is a preventive visit specifically designed to create a personalized prevention plan, review your health history, and screen for common age-related conditions. <strong>It's covered 100% by Medicare</strong> — meaning $0 out of pocket in most cases.</p>

<p>IWC Primary Care offers these visits and helps patients navigate the process so they get the full value of this free benefit every year.</p>
