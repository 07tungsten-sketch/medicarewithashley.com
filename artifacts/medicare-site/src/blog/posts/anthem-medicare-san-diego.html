Anthem also sells Medigap plans in California. These plans supplement Original Medicare by covering costs like deductibles, copays, and coinsurance — giving you predictable out-of-pocket expenses. Anthem's Medigap premiums are competitive, but because Medigap benefits are federally standardized by plan letter, comparing prices across carriers is always worthwhile.
