Aetna Medicare Advantage in San Diego

Aetna offers Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans in San Diego County, including both HMO and PPO options. Medicare Advantage plans through Aetna replace Original Medicare Parts A and B and typically include:

  • Prescription drug coverage (Part D) built into the plan
  • Dental, vision, and hearing benefits
  • Fitness benefits, often through SilverSneakers
  • Over-the-counter allowances for health products
  • $0 or low monthly premiums on many plans

Aetna's Provider Network in San Diego

Aetna's Medicare Advantage networks in San Diego County include a range of local physicians, specialists, and hospital systems. If you have a preferred doctor or health system, verifying they're in-network with Aetna before enrolling is essential — especially with HMO plans, which generally require in-network care except in emergencies.

Aetna PPO plans offer more flexibility, allowing you to see out-of-network providers at a higher cost share. This can matter if you travel frequently or split time between San Diego and another state.

Aetna Medicare Supplement Plans

Aetna also offers Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans in California. Medigap plans work alongside Original Medicare to cover costs like copays, coinsurance, and deductibles. Aetna's Medigap premiums vary by plan type and your age at enrollment — comparing quotes across multiple carriers, including Aetna, is the right approach since the benefits on each lettered plan are standardized by law.

Aetna Part D Prescription Drug Plans

For those who stay on Original Medicare and want standalone prescription coverage, Aetna offers Part D drug plans in San Diego. Comparing formularies — the list of covered drugs — and checking your specific medications before choosing a Part D plan can save you significant money throughout the year.
